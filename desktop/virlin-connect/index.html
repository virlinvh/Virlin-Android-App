Pairing steps

  1. Open Virlin → Apps → Virlin Link on the phone.
  2. Tap Open scanner and approve this computer.
  3. Keep Virlin Link open while testing the connection.

Wi-Fi pairing currently verifies a secure connection only; desktop editing and mirroring are not available through QR yet. Allow this app on private networks if Windows asks.

USB setup

  1. Enable Android Developer options and USB debugging on your phone.
  2. Connect the phone by USB, unlock it, and approve the phone’s USB debugging prompt for this computer.
  3. Choose Open Virlin when your phone appears above.

USB opens a live mirrored window for mouse, keyboard, scrolling, and paste. Nothing is copied from the phone. If USB debugging is disabled, a cable alone cannot grant app control.
