Pairing steps

  1. Open Virlin → Apps → Virlin Link on the phone.
  2. Tap Open scanner and approve this computer.
  3. Keep Virlin open while working with projects; you can navigate away from Virlin Link.

Project browsing, basic creation, and hierarchy bulk import are available here. Notes, files, execution controls, and mirroring are not available through QR yet. Allow this app on private networks if Windows asks.

Pair over the cable

  1. Connect your unlocked phone to this computer by USB.
  2. On the phone, open Settings → Network & internet → Hotspot & tethering and turn on USB tethering. Your phone may use mobile data; check your carrier plan.
  3. When the USB network is detected above, show the QR and scan it in Virlin → Apps → Virlin Link.

This is the same secure project workspace as Wi-Fi, carried over the cable. It does not mirror the screen. Android requires you to turn on tethering yourself.

Advanced mirror setup

  1. Enable Android Developer options and USB debugging on your phone.
  2. Connect the phone by USB, unlock it, and approve the phone’s USB debugging prompt for this computer.
  3. Choose Open Virlin when your phone appears above.

This mirror supports mouse, keyboard, scrolling, and paste. A cable alone cannot grant screen control, and Virlin Connect cannot enable debugging for you.
