First time connecting?

  1. Install Virlin on your Android phone.
  2. Turn on Developer options → USB debugging on the phone.
  3. Connect it by USB and accept the phone’s Allow USB debugging prompt.
  4. Select your phone above and choose Open Virlin.

The mirroring window supports your mouse, keyboard, scrolling, and clipboard paste. Disconnecting does not remove anything from the phone.
